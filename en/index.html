Your files at home.

DogNAS turns an Android you already own into private storage for your home: your files stay with you, on your Wi-Fi, with no cloud and no monthly fee.

How it works

1

Start the server

Install DogNAS on the Android that will keep your files — on the device, an SD card or a USB drive — and tap Start.

2

Connect your phone

Generate a profile's QR Code and scan it with DogNAS Lite. Each profile has its own PIN and space.

3

Use your files

Browse, upload and download over your home Wi-Fi. PCs and Macs connect via SFTP.

No cloud

Files travel only between your own devices, on your network, encrypted.

Profiles for the family

Each profile has its own space and its own PIN.

No account, no ads

No sign-up, no ads, no data collection.

That phone sitting in a drawer becomes your home storage.
