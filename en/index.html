Your files at home.

DogNAS turns an Android you already own into private storage for your home: your files stay with you, on your Wi-Fi, with no cloud and no monthly fee.

No cloud

Files travel only between your own devices, on your network, encrypted.

Profiles for the family

Each profile has its own space and its own PIN.

Connect in seconds

Phones with a QR Code through DogNAS Lite; PCs and Macs via SFTP.

Free to start

64 GB free. DogNAS Pro unlocks everything, as a one-time purchase.

Two apps

DogNAS

The server: install it on the Android that will keep your files.

DogNAS Lite

The access app: install it on your phone to browse, upload and download.
