DogNAS Client

The full app to reach the files on your DogNAS, on Android and iPhone, over your home Wi-Fi. No cloud, no account.

Google Play · Coming soonApp Store · Coming soon

Not in the stores yet. Meanwhile, DogNAS Client Lite already reaches your DogNAS on Android.

What it does

Pair with a QR code

Point the camera at the profile’s QR code on the server and enter the PIN. Each profile has its own space.

Browse and organize

See your folders; create, rename and delete files right on your DogNAS.

Upload and download

Send photos, videos and documents to the server and download them back, with a transfer queue.

Only on your network

Files stay on your DogNAS Android and travel over your home Wi-Fi, encrypted (SSH/SFTP). Nothing goes through a cloud.

Free, with Pro on the server

DogNAS Client is free. The limits and Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription) belong to the DogNAS server: Free means 64 GB, Home + 2 profiles and 3 devices; Pro means the whole device and transfers that keep going with the app closed.

Requirements

A DogNAS server on the same Wi-Fi network. Android and iPhone (iOS 15 or newer).

What about Client Lite?

DogNAS Client Lite stays available on Google Play: it is the light app, ideal for simpler devices.
