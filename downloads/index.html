Step 1 · On the old or idle Android

DogNAS (server)

Install this app on the old or idle Android that will stay at home. It becomes the home storage and keeps everyone’s files.

What you get: private storage at home, on an Android that was sitting idle.

  • Turn an idle Android into the home storage
  • Connect the family’s phones with a QR Code and PIN, no account
  • One profile per person, each with its own space and PIN
  • Store on the device itself, an SD card or a USB drive
  • Also works on an Android tablet or TV box
Download the server

Install it on the device that stays at home, powered on and on the Wi-Fi. Android 7.0 or newer. Start on the Free plan; Pro is optional (one-time purchase, no subscription).

Step 2 · On your everyday phone

DogNAS Client

Install this app on the phone you use every day. It connects to the DogNAS server (the old Android at home) over Wi-Fi: you move files there and open the ones stored there.

What you get: free space on your everyday phone, with your files kept on DogNAS.

  • Move photos and videos from your phone to DogNAS and free up space on it
  • Scan paper with the camera and save it as PDF or JPEG straight to DogNAS
  • Open photos, videos, music and PDFs stored on DogNAS without downloading first
  • If the Wi-Fi drops, the upload continues where it stopped
  • Start organized with ready-made folders: Home, Work and Minimal
Download the Client

Needs the DogNAS server (step 1) running on another Android, on the same Wi-Fi network. Android 7.0 or newer.

Step 2 · Light alternative to the Client

DogNAS Client Lite

Does the same job as the Client: it runs on your phone and reaches the DogNAS server. It is simpler and lighter, good for an older phone or one with little space.

What you get: access to your DogNAS files even on a simple or older phone.

  • Reach your DogNAS files on a simpler phone or one with little space
  • Connect in seconds with the server’s QR Code
  • Start with ready-made folder templates
  • See how much space your profile already uses on the server
  • Download files from DogNAS to your phone when you need them
Download the Client Lite

Needs the DogNAS server (step 1) running on another Android, on the same Wi-Fi network. Android 7.0 or newer.

Client or Client Lite?

Both run on your phone and reach the same server. The Client is the full app (scan documents, view files without downloading, thumbnails); the Client Lite is lighter and simpler. Pick one, or install both.
